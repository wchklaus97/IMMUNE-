# IMMUNE privacy notice — publisher draft

Last technical review: 2026-09-01

IMMUNE is an offline single-player demo. The current build does not include
accounts, advertising, analytics, telemetry, multiplayer, chat, user-generated
content, remote configuration, or in-game purchases. It does not transmit
personal data to the developer, publisher, an AI service, or an advertising
provider.

The game stores settings, campaign progress, and research unlocks locally on the
player's device through Godot's per-user application-data directory. Removing
the game may not remove these local files, depending on the operating system and
Steam uninstall behaviour. Players can delete the local save data themselves.

Steam may independently process account, purchase, cloud, crash, or platform
data under Valve's own terms. This build does not integrate Steam Cloud,
Steamworks telemetry, or a developer-operated backend.

Before publication, the publisher must add its legal entity/name, support
contact, effective date, jurisdiction-specific language, and a stable public
URL. If any SDK, online feature, crash reporter, analytics service, or Steam
Cloud support is added, this draft must be re-audited before upload.
