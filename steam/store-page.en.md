## Short description

Command six jelly immune-cell families through three-phase lane-defense
missions, unleash signature active skills, and grow a permanent research
network.

## About this game

The system is under siege. Build a permanent immune research network, choose
the cell family that fits the threat, and enter a living battlefield where
every mission escalates from defense to expedition to a final boss assault.

IMMUNE combines accessible lane-defense action with light strategy. Hold the
Immune Core during the opening wave, switch into a mobile or relay duty to
cleanse the front line, then survive Total War against a mission-specific
pathogen boss. Every family has its own combat profile and a cooldown-based
active skill, while every mission adds a distinct encounter pattern.

### Features

- Play six immune-cell families: T Cell, B Cell, Macrophage, NK Cell, Antibody
  Construct, and Dendritic Cell.
- Fight through six missions with distinct pacing events, pathogen traits, and
  bosses.
- Master a three-phase combat loop: Core Defense, Expedition, and Total War.
- Use family-specific active skills, switch duties, and inspect live cell intel.
- Invest rewards across a permanent 200-node research network.
- Play in English or Traditional Chinese with keyboard/mouse or controller.
- Tune audio, screen shake, reduced motion, and runtime language from the pause
  menu.

## Controls summary

- Move during Expedition: WASD / left stick
- Active skill: E / right bumper
- Switch duty: Space / controller action
- Cell intel: R / controller action
- Pause: Escape / controller menu
